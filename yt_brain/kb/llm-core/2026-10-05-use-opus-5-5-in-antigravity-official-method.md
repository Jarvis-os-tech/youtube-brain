# Use Opus 5.5 In Antigravity (Official Method)

## Core Concept
The video announces that Claude Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5 have arrived in Antigravity, each with low, medium, and high modes, and shows that they can be enabled and used from the same account that previously only exposed Gemini-class models and the older, unpopular Claude Opus 4.6 — with access tied to a paid Anthropic Pro/Ultra (or "Gemini Pro") subscription, since the free plan is excluded.

## Key Takeaways
- Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5 are now available in Antigravity; the speaker received access and is demonstrating the "official method" to get it.
- Before this, Antigravity only had Gemini-class models plus a "such old model of Claude," Opus 4.6, described as "not at all fun to use."
- Each of the two models exposes exactly three modes: **low**, **medium**, and **high**.
- The speaker verified the models work by selecting Claude 5.5 and sending a simple prompt: "Hello friend. How are you?" — the model replied "I am doing great, friend. Thanks, how are you?"
- The official source is the **Claude Updates X handle**, which posted that Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5 are available in Anthropic.
- Availability statement cited from that post: the models are available on **paid Pro and Ultra plans** — not available on the free plan.
- Anyone with a Pro plan can open Anthropic and simply see the models; the speaker frames this as a benefit for people who lacked Claude Pro access, who can now reach Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5 here.
- The speaker repeatedly invites comments on whether users wanted GPT-o 1 in Antigravity instead, and asks viewers to subscribe for AI updates.
